# Image Editor Plugin — Changelog

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-11

### Added

- **File Manager Integration** - An "Edit image" button in the file manager opens a full-screen editor for JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF files.
- **Crop, Rotate And Resize** - Free or fixed-ratio crop, 90° rotation, flips, and resizing with optional locked proportions.
- **Adjustments And Filters** - Brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, blur and sharpen, plus eight one-click filters.
- **Annotations** - Text, rectangles, ellipses, lines, arrows and a freehand pen, editable after drawing.
- **Watermark** - Text or image watermark, placed or tiled, with size and opacity.
- **Undo, Redo And Compare** - Step through your edits, reset, or hold to compare with the original.
- **Save Options** - Replace the original or save a copy as PNG, JPEG or WebP with a quality setting.
