# Definitions Plugin — Changelog

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-09

### Added

- **Glossary** - Add terms and definitions in the admin; they are underlined in your content with an accessible popup on hover, tap or keyboard focus.
- **Variants** - Plurals and related phrasing trigger the same definition, and the popup shows the form that appears in the text.
- **Occurrence Limits** - Underline every occurrence or only the first few, set globally and per definition.
- **Headings Option** - Never underline terms inside titles and headings.
- **Glossary Table** - Search, status filter, per-page choice (10, 25, 50, All) and pagination.
- **Settings Tab** - Global options on their own tab, like the other official plugins.
